HOIT / Treatment documents

One treatment. Invoice and receipt.
Client data stays in this browser

1. Bring in a treatment

Paste spreadsheet cells, a copied table, or a Markdown table with | separators. Include all 17 columns, from # through Address. Formatting and separator lines are handled automatically.

Using a screenshot?

On your Mac, open the screenshot in Preview and use Live Text to copy text. For reliable column matching, copy cells directly from the tracker. Image text is not automatically extracted here. You can paste or select a screenshot as a local reference, then fill the fields below.

2. Choose a client

Select a saved client, then apply their details to this treatment. Your treatment date, fees and payment stay as entered.

Client details

Saving changes updates the directory only. To update the current treatment too, click “Use client for treatment”. Older treatments retain their original details.

Save an encrypted session after adding or editing clients. Reopen that file next time to restore your directory. There is no automatic saving.

3. Review treatment

A “y” in the tracker is a flag, not a payment amount. Confirm the amount and payment date before creating a receipt.

Practitioner & document settings

Tax rates follow your supplied template and are editable. Confirm they apply to the treatment. Add your correct credentials and registration numbers before issuing documents.

Save, reopen & privacy

This website serves the app code. Client details you enter or open from an encrypted file are processed in this tab and are not sent to GitHub by the app. GitHub can receive ordinary website access information. Nothing is automatically stored by this app. Closing, reloading or iOS unloading this page can clear the session. Save your encrypted session before switching apps or leaving the page. Opening the website on another device does not sync your data. Keep your existing tracker as your record of issued document numbers and payments.

Encrypted sessions include your client directory, treatment data and settings. Older session files also open, with an empty directory if they did not include one. Keep your password safe: there is no password recovery. Settings files contain practitioner details only and are not encrypted.

PDFs are ordinary, unencrypted documents. Save and send them using your approved secure workflow. This app does not send email or update your tracker. Running locally alone does not establish HIPAA or other privacy-law compliance. Browser extensions, device access, backups and PDF handling still matter.

Screenshot support is reference-only. For automatic entry, paste spreadsheet cells or a pipe-separated table. After the page loads, the app makes no network requests for client data, OCR, analytics or fonts. An internet connection is needed to load the website; offline installation is not included.

On a computer, choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog. On iPhone, tap Save receipt PDF, open the print preview, then use Share to save or send the PDF. Turn off browser headers and footers when available. Review the recipient in your chosen sending app.
